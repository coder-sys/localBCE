gate_verify.exe: the gate's Lean decisions for the producer's publish check and the payer's index, fork and release checks

Lean/Lake executable `gate_verify` (root BlindLedger.Gate.VerifyCli), built from the
`lean/` sources whose manifest is recorded in tools/tcb_report.txt.
One exe, five mode-byte frames (the stateless frame is deleted), run once per request or resident as `gate_verify serve`
(loop of `u32 len | mode byte + frame` requests, each answered only from its own bytes as `u32 len | exit-code byte | line`; a malformed
request exits 2 so the caller sees EOF); exit 0 for ACCEPT, INDEX, STORED
and FORK, 1 for REJECT and HOLD, 2 for a malformed frame (stderr `GATE_VERIFY=FRAME`):
- 0x01 PUBLISH: `root_pk || u32|cert || u32|last (0 = genesis) || u32|signed new || u16 k || (u32|token)^k || u64 tpm`.
- 0x07 CUSTODY: `u8 written || u64 tpm || u8 has_store || u64 newest`; prints `GATE_VERIFY=CUSTODY create|ready|catch_up` (exit 0) or `no_store|counter_reset|store_rewound|store_ahead` (exit 1).
- 0x02 INDEX: `u32|signed checkpoint`; prints `GATE_VERIFY=INDEX <tenant hex> shard epoch from to <digest hex> <prev hex>`.
- 0x03 STORE: `root_pk || u16 m || certs || u16 j || candidates || u16 f || fork evidence || u32|new`.
- 0x05 RELEASE: `root_pk || u16 m || certs || u16 f || fork evidence || u32|checkpoint || u64 now || u16 g || grants ||
  u8 released || u8 pinned [|| 32-byte key] || u32|DECIDE-REQUEST || u32|token`, a grant being `u16|tenant ident || u16|id ||
  u16 n || (u16|rule version)^n || u16 n || (u16|engine release)^n`; answers ACCEPT or HOLD <first failure>. A 0x04 frame (the
  grant without a tenant) is malformed.
ML-DSA-87 (FIPS 204 algorithm) verified by Lean code written from the standard
(Gate/MlDsa87.lean), not by AWS-LC; no validation is claimed.

commit tools/tcb_report.txt (it requires the in-tree build to equal the pins and the report's gate_engine line).
