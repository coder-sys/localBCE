  failing with nothing written when READS reads (65,536) do not reach its end; check it; cut an end after the last commit point (a run-end or
  repair record) where the last command's own records run past the end of the log into `LOG.cut-<record>` and write a repair
  record; a log changed at or before its last commit point or before its end, or whose last command is whole and wrong, is
  refused with `CORRUPT` and left as it is), apply the commands of FRAMES in order to a shard that can dispatch when DISPATCH is
  1, append their records, sync the log, read back and check only the records from the last good one on, from the checker's
  state there, and only then write a run-end record (it holds the SHA3-512 of the run's bytes, chained to the digest of the run-end record before it, which a later check recomputes) and sync it (unless the log already ends at a run-end record; a repair record does not count), write one
  answer for each command to a synced temporary file, close the log and rename the file to ANSWERS (when the check fails the
  run's records are set aside in `LOG.cut-<record>` and cut off and no answer is written; when the log cannot be closed no
