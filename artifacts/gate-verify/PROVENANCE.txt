gate_verify.exe: the gate's Lean decisions for the producer's publish check and the payer's index, fork and release checks

Lean/Lake executable `gate_verify` (root BlindLedger.Gate.VerifyCli), built from the
`lean/` sources whose manifest is recorded in tools/tcb_report.txt.
One exe, four mode-byte frames (the stateless frame is deleted); exit 0 for ACCEPT, INDEX, STORED
and FORK, 1 for REJECT and HOLD, 2 for a malformed frame (stderr `GATE_VERIFY=FRAME`):
- 0x01 PUBLISH: `root_pk || u32|cert || u32|last (0 = genesis) || u32|signed new || u16 k || (u32|token)^k`.
- 0x02 INDEX: `u32|signed checkpoint`; prints `GATE_VERIFY=INDEX <tenant hex> shard epoch from to <digest hex> <prev hex>`.
- 0x03 STORE: `root_pk || u16 m || certs || u16 j || candidates || u16 f || fork evidence || u32|new`.
- 0x04 RELEASE: `root_pk || u16 m || certs || u16 f || fork evidence || u32|checkpoint || u64 now || u16 g || grants ||
  u8 released || u8 pinned [|| 32-byte key] || u32|DECIDE-REQUEST || u32|token`; answers ACCEPT or HOLD <first failure>.
ML-DSA-87 (FIPS 204 algorithm) verified by Lean code written from the standard
(Gate/MlDsa87.lean), not by AWS-LC; no validation is claimed.

commit tools/tcb_report.txt (it requires the in-tree build to equal the pins).
