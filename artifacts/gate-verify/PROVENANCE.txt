gate_verify.exe: the payer-side verifier for gate checkpoints (PQ signing M1)

Lean/Lake executable `gate_verify` (root BlindLedger.Gate.VerifyCli), built from
`lean/` at commit e6455e4 (git tree id of lean/ cacac9bdcb380512425d54c4c2ccb806dafd06e1).
Stateless: input `root_pk (2592) || u32 BE cert length || KEY-CERT || signed checkpoint`;
output `GATE_VERIFY=ACCEPT` (exit 0) or `REJECT` (exit 1); exit 2 for malformed input.
